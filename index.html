    The Time Capsule ⏳

    A letter to a day we haven't reached yet

    To us, later

    If you're reading this, the years did what years do. They went fast, and we let them.

    Here's what I want you to remember from the early days: it was never the big moments. It was the sofa, the sign on the shelf, the café table, the sunset we kept driving out to. It was choosing the same person again on the unremarkable Tuesdays.

    Whatever has been added to the wall since I wrote this, good. That was the whole plan. Keep the clock running and keep filing the days.

    — Fred
